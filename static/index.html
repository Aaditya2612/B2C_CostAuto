Assumptions & notes taken from the sheets

  • Only carriers that have a commercial sheet in the B2C workbook are quoted: Delhivery, Blue Dart (Dart Plus), DTDC, Ekart, Shadowfax, Velocity Express, Amazon, Elastic Run. The workbook's Carrier List flags are not used.
  • Delhivery Surface (W.E.F 01-02-2024): forward is a flat per-shipment rate (additional weight slabs are Rs 0 on the card). Zones A/B use X/Y, which depend on the monthly volume slab: 1-3 Lakh → X=35, Y=42; ≥3 Lakh → X=31, Y=38.5. The <1 Lakh slab says "Existing Rate Card, Table B", whose X/Y are not printed in the workbook, so A/B forward is not quoted for that slab — choose a higher slab to quote it. Zones C1–F are the same for every slab; RTO is Rs 0; DTO is first 500 g + add-on per 500 g; Guwahati B_SPL is Rs 42/shipment. The SDD/NDD entries on the sheet (Delhivery_MM / Delhivery_NDD columns) are separate products and not quoted.
  • Blue Dart (Dart Plus): min chargeable 1 kg; then every additional 500 g slab. RTO = 70% of forward rates. Volume discounts apply on freight. Cash-on-delivery fee (1.2% or min INR 15) is not included — it depends on the collectible amount, not on weight. Rates valid till 31-Dec-2025.
  • DTDC (w.e.f 21-May-2025): flat Rs 34.9 for first 5,000 g, additional 1,000 g at Rs 0, across all categories; forward movement only. A special "Siliguri DS MFC location Rs 40 (from Feb 2026)" note is not applied — no warehouse in the list is at Siliguri.
  • Ekart: base Rs 41 for 0–5 kg then Rs 7 per 500 g, inclusive of forward, COD & RTO (no separate DTO). The volume-discount table from the sheet is selectable ("From Jul'26 onwards": ≥2,00,000/mo Rs 38, ≥4,00,000/mo Rs 37; the May'26–Jun'26 transitional numbers are noted in the sheet).
  • Shadowfax (W.E.F 01-06-2025): per-shipment by category (A Intracity, B1 Intrastate, B2 Within-zone/Regional, C Metro/ROI/SZ, Kerala LM). The ≥4 L shipments/month tier applies discounted rates. The "+Rs 2.5/shipment for SFX MM in Kerala" and the separate "RVP with QC" reverse-pickup product are not modeled.
  • Velocity Express: per-shipment by zone — Only LM 29, Local 31, Intrastate 36, Intra Region 40 (incl. carrying cost).
  • Amazon: CPS per shipment plus cumulative monthly discount; add-on INR 15/kg for every full kg above 2 kg, with the same discount applied on the add-on rate.
  • Elastic Run: Rs 33/32/31 by average monthly volume; SDD at Rs 37 per delivered order for WH 2, 4, 10, 12, 28; NDD–Regional at Rs 40 per delivered order with no return freight.
  • Not-served lanes come from the Zone Master sentinels (NA / * / blank). The weight you enter is treated as the billing/chargeable weight — no volumetric (LBH/5000) conversion is applied.
