Notes

  • Only carriers that have a commercial sheet in the B2C workbook are quoted: Delhivery, Blue Dart (Dart Plus), DTDC, Ekart, Shadowfax, Amazon, Elastic Run. The workbook's Carrier List flags are not used.
  • Delhivery Surface (W.E.F 01-02-2024): forward is a flat per-shipment rate (additional weight slabs are Rs 0 on the card). Zones A/B use X/Y, which depend on the monthly volume slab: 1-3 Lakh → X=35, Y=42; ≥3 Lakh → X=31, Y=38.5. The retired <1 Lakh slab ("Existing Rate Card, Table B") is not offered. Zones C1–F are the same for every slab; RTO is Rs 0; Guwahati B_SPL is Rs 42/shipment. The SDD/NDD entries on the sheet (Delhivery_MM / Delhivery_NDD columns) are separate products and not quoted.
  • Blue Dart (Dart Plus): min chargeable 1 kg; then every additional 500 g slab. RTO = 70% of forward rates. Volume discounts (up to 10% on freight, by monthly-shipment tier) apply. Cash-on-delivery fee (1.2% or min INR 15) is not included — it depends on the collectible amount, not on weight. Rates valid till 31-Dec-2025.
  • DTDC (w.e.f 21-May-2025): flat Rs 34.9 for first 5,000 g, additional 1,000 g at Rs 0, across all categories; forward movement only. The sheet's "Siliguri DS (QUIK & SDD, W.E.F Feb 2026) Rs 40" special is applied to destination pincodes in the Siliguri region (prefix 734).
  • Ekart: base Rs 41 for 0–5 kg then Rs 7 per 500 g, inclusive of forward, COD & RTO (no separate DTO). The volume-discount table from the sheet is selectable ("From Jul'26 onwards": ≥2,00,000/mo Rs 38, ≥4,00,000/mo Rs 37). The May'26–Jun'26 transitional tiers are noted in the sheet but not offered here.
  • Shadowfax (W.E.F 01-06-2025): per-shipment by category (A Intracity Rs 33, B1 Intrastate Rs 35.5, B2 Within-zone/Regional Rs 38.5, C Metro/ROI/Special Zone Rs 42.5, Kerala Only-LM Rs 31). The ≥4 L shipments/month tier applies discounted rates (B1 Intrastate Rs 33.5, B2 Within-zone/Regional Rs 36.5). The separate "RVP with QC" reverse-pickup product (W.E.F 01-04-2025) is quoted via the "+ Shadowfax RVP" checkbox: CPS by RVP category (Local 35 / Regional 50 / Metro 60 / ROI 80 / SZ 90) + QC Rs 15/shipment (pass or fail); RVP category is derived from the lane's Shadowfax forward zone (Intracity→Local, Intrastate/Within-Zone→Regional, Metro→Metro, ROI→ROI, Special Zone→SZ). Liability terms are informational (max Rs 5,000 on RVP lost shipments; RVP QC liability 20% of invoice value). The "+Rs 2.5/shipment for SFX MM in Kerala" is not modeled.
  • Amazon: CPS per shipment plus cumulative monthly discount (Rs 40 → Rs 34.4, down to 14% off). First 2 kg included, then INR 15 per full extra kg, with the same discount applied on the add-on rate.
  • Elastic Run: no lane column exists in the zone master, so it quotes only from the warehouses with a scheme on the card — SDD (local) Rs 37 per delivered order for WH 2, 4, 10, 12, 28 (W.E.F 01-07-2025) and NDD–Regional Rs 40 per delivered order (W.E.F 20-02-2026; no return freight, billed on delivered orders only). Other warehouses (incl. WH 16, 21, 25, 29, 40, 42, 45, 47, 6032) are shown as not served so no cost is fabricated. "Local" = the destination pin falls in the warehouse's SDD metro area (per-WH pincode prefixes in the price card); anything else on those 5 warehouses is Regional. The pre-2025 flat Rs 33/32/31 volume scheme is retired.
  • Not-served lanes come from the Zone Master sentinels (NA / * / blank). The weight you enter is treated as the billing/chargeable weight — no volumetric (LBH/5000) conversion is applied.
