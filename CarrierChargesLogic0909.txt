Delhivery — Flat fee per shipment (extra weight is free). Zones C1–F are fixed (₹55–₹92). Zones A/B depend on monthly volume: 1–3 Lakh → A=₹35, B=₹42; ≥3 Lakh → A=₹31, B=₹38.5. For <1 Lakh the price isn't printed in the workbook, so A/B can't be quoted on that setting. RTO = ₹0. DTO = ₹44–₹74 first 500 g, then ₹40–₹71 per extra 500 g. Guwahati B_SPL = ₹42.

Blue Dart (Dart Plus) — Min charge: 1 kg, then every extra 500 g costs more (₹5–₹21 depending on zone). RTO = 70% of forward. Volume discount (up to 10%) applies on freight. COD fee not included. Rates valid till 31-Dec-2025.

DTDC — Always ₹34.9 per shipment (first 5 kg; extra weight free). Forward only.

Ekart — ₹41 for first 5 kg, then ₹7 per 500 g. Price already includes forward + COD + RTO. Big-volume discount: ≥2,00,000/mo → ₹38, ≥4,00,000/mo → ₹37.

Shadowfax — Flat fee by category: Intracity ₹33, Intrastate ₹35.5, Within-Zone/Regional ₹38.5, Metro/ROI/Special ₹42.5. At ≥4 L shipments/month, Intrastate & Within-Zone drop to ₹33.5 / ₹36.5.

Velocity Express — Flat fee by zone: Only-LM ₹29, Local ₹31, Intrastate ₹36, Intra-Region/Regional ₹40.

Amazon — CPS by monthly volume (₹40 → ₹34.4, down to 14% discount). First 2 kg included, then ₹15 per full extra kg, same discount applied.

Elastic Run — ₹33 by default; ₹32/₹31 at higher monthly volume. ₹37 if the warehouse is one of WH 2/4/10/12/28 (SDD). ₹40 for NDD-Regional.
